# PRODUCT.md — Taylored & Beaded

## What this is

A showcase website for Taylored & Beaded, a boutique handmade beaded jewelry studio. One maker: Taylor, who started the brand after unexpectedly losing her job and turning beading from stress relief into a passion business. Every piece is one-of-a-kind, hand-strung in small batches. The site displays the work, tells her story, and routes purchase interest to an inquiry form. There is no cart or checkout; payment happens personally after an inquiry.

## Register

brand

## Users

- Women roughly 16–45 discovering the brand through Instagram (@taylored_beaded) and word of mouth; they arrive already charmed and want to browse, pick a piece, and reach out.
- Gift buyers (moms, friends, boyfriends) who need confidence the piece will arrive beautifully packaged and feel special.
- Local/community supporters following Taylor's story who may not buy today but subscribe and share.

## Brand & tone

- Handmade, heartfelt, feminine, optimistic. "Here's to turning setbacks into something beautiful."
- Personal and warm, never corporate. The maker's voice ("Hi, I'm Taylor") is a feature, not a bio page afterthought.
- Premium boutique feel on a handmade budget: think small-studio editorial, not mass-market Shopify template.
- Key taglines: "Jewelry that tells your story." / "Handmade. Heartfelt. Timeless." / "Made to inspire & empower."
- Value props: handmade with love; quality materials that last; beautifully packaged & gift ready; made to inspire & empower.

## Catalog & pricing reality

- Categories: Necklaces ($25), Bracelets ($15), Anklets ($15), Bag Charms ($15). Flat category pricing with occasional per-piece overrides.
- Pieces are one-of-a-kind; sold pieces stay visible marked "Sold".
- Bundle deals and payment methods (Cash App/Zelle) are deliberately NOT shown on the site; Taylor handles them in inquiry replies.

## Anti-references

- Generic Shopify/Etsy template look; anything that reads "dropshipping store".
- Loud e-commerce urgency (sale banners, countdown timers, discount popups).
- Sterile minimalism that erases the handmade warmth; the site should feel like her studio table, elevated.
- Flashy/bouncy animation; motion must be soft, slow, and subtle.

## Strategic principles

1. The photography (colorful beads against warm neutrals) is the hero; the design frames it with generous cream space.
2. Taylor's story is the differentiator against mass-produced jewelry; weave it through the homepage, not just /about.
3. Every dead end becomes an invitation: sold piece → inquire about a similar custom; empty category → newsletter signup.
4. Trust for gift buyers: packaging, materials, and care information visible and reassuring.
